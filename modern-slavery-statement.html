Modern Slavery Statement

Last updated: 21 November 2026

HomeModern Slavery Statement

Perfictus is committed to preventing modern slavery, forced labour, child labour and human trafficking in our business and in our supply chain. We publish this statement voluntarily, in the spirit of the UK Modern Slavery Act 2015 and similar laws, because we work with clients in the UK, the USA, the GCC and India.

Our business

Perfictus is a digital and e-commerce agency based in India, serving clients in Kuwait, the GCC, India, the United Kingdom and the United States. We provide Shopify development, mobile app development, SEO, digital marketing, design, video editing and remote developer services.

Our supply chain

Our supply chain is short and low risk. It mainly consists of:

  • Our own team and remote professionals (developers, designers and editors)
  • Software and cloud providers such as Shopify, Google, GitHub, Apple and app development tools
  • Office, equipment and professional service suppliers

Our policies

  • Fair employment: everyone who works with us does so freely, with written terms, agreed working hours and fair pay paid on time
  • No forced or child labour: we do not use forced, bonded or child labour, and we do not hold identity documents or charge recruitment fees
  • Equal opportunity: as our careers page says, we hire on talent and skills, and we do not discriminate
  • Speaking up: anyone can raise concerns confidentially without fear of retaliation

Due diligence and risk

Because most of our work is digital and our suppliers are established technology companies, we consider the risk of modern slavery in our operations to be low. We still:

  • Choose reputable suppliers and partners
  • Expect our suppliers to follow laws on employment, wages and human rights
  • Review any concern that is reported to us and take action, including ending a relationship if needed

Training and awareness

Our leadership is responsible for this statement. We make sure the people who hire team members and choose suppliers understand the signs of modern slavery and know how to report a concern.

Reporting a concern

If you have a concern about modern slavery connected to Perfictus, please email info@perfictus.com. Every report is treated confidentially.

We review this statement every year. It has been approved by the management of Perfictus.
