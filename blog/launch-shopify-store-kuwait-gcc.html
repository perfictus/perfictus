How to Launch a Shopify Store in Kuwait and the GCC (2026 Guide)

Perfictus Shopify TeamOctober 10, 2026 3 min read

Selling online in Kuwait and the GCC needs local payments, Arabic support and fast delivery. Here is how to set up Shopify the right way.

E-commerce across Kuwait and the wider GCC keeps growing, with shoppers who are mobile-first, brand-aware and used to fast delivery. Shopify is a great platform for the region, but a successful launch needs a few local details right from day one.

1. Local payment methods

Payment options make or break conversion in the GCC. Shoppers in Kuwait expect KNET, alongside cards and Apple Pay. Popular options include:

  • MyFatoorah or Tap Payments to accept KNET, cards and Apple Pay.
  • Buy now, pay later such as Tabby and Tamara, which are popular in the region.
  • Cash on delivery (COD), still important for first-time customers in many markets.

Check which payment providers support your country and business type before you design checkout. Availability differs between Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Bahrain and Oman.

2. Arabic and English (RTL)

A bilingual store builds trust. Use Shopify’s languages features with a theme that fully supports right-to-left (RTL) layouts, and translate product titles, descriptions, policies and emails properly rather than relying only on automatic translation.

3. Currencies and markets

Use Shopify Markets to show prices in KWD, SAR, AED, QAR, BHD and OMR, set market-specific pricing and control which products are available where.

4. Shipping and delivery

  • Offer same-day or next-day delivery in your home city if you can.
  • Show delivery times clearly on product pages and in checkout.
  • Use clear address fields, with block, street and building details for Kuwait.

5. Marketing that works in the region

  • Instagram, Snapchat and TikTok drive a large share of discovery.
  • WhatsApp is the preferred channel for questions and order updates.
  • Plan campaigns around Ramadan, Eid, White Friday and national days.

Launch checklist

  1. Choose a theme with full RTL support and mobile-first design.
  2. Set up local payment gateways and test every method.
  3. Configure Markets, currencies and shipping zones.
  4. Translate content and notifications into Arabic.
  5. Add WhatsApp contact and order updates.
  6. Place test orders from every country you serve.

Building trust with GCC shoppers

  • Show a local phone number and WhatsApp contact.
  • Display clear delivery times, returns and exchange policies in Arabic and English.
  • Use real reviews and social proof from local customers.
  • Highlight secure payment options like KNET and Apple Pay at the top of checkout.
  • Offer gift wrapping and gift messages, popular during Ramadan and Eid.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use Shopify in Kuwait?

Yes. Shopify works in Kuwait and across the GCC. Merchants typically use third-party payment gateways such as MyFatoorah or Tap Payments to accept KNET and cards.

Does Shopify support Arabic?

Yes. Shopify supports Arabic storefronts, and many themes support right-to-left layouts. Choose a theme with proper RTL support and translate content professionally for the best results.

Should I offer cash on delivery?

For many GCC brands, yes, at least at first. COD lowers the barrier for first-time buyers. You can limit it by order value or region to reduce failed deliveries.
