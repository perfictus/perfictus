Agentic Commerce on Shopify: How to Get Your Products Sold by AI Assistants

Perfictus Shopify TeamNovember 14, 2026 3 min read

More shoppers are asking AI assistants what to buy. Here is how agentic commerce works on Shopify and how to make your catalog AI-ready.

Search is changing. Instead of typing keywords into Google and clicking ten links, more shoppers now ask an AI assistant: “Find me a waterproof hiking jacket under $150 that ships to Kuwait.” The assistant searches, compares and, increasingly, completes the purchase. This is called agentic commerce.

Shopify has made agentic commerce a central part of its 2026 roadmap. Eligible products can appear on AI shopping surfaces, and Shop Pay can process checkout inside those experiences. For merchants, that is a new sales channel that rewards one thing above all: clear, complete product data.

How AI assistants choose products

An AI agent cannot “see” your beautiful banner. It reads structured information: product titles, descriptions, attributes, prices, availability, reviews and policies. When two products are similar, the one with clearer, more trustworthy data usually wins.

  • Relevance: does the product clearly match what the shopper asked for?
  • Completeness: are size, material, colour, compatibility and use cases spelled out?
  • Trust: are prices, stock levels, shipping times and returns accurate and consistent?
  • Social proof: are there genuine reviews and ratings?

7 steps to make your Shopify catalog AI-ready

  1. Write descriptive titles. “Men’s Waterproof Hiking Jacket – Lightweight, Packable, Navy” beats “Explorer Jacket”.
  2. Answer real questions in descriptions. Who is it for, what problem does it solve, how does it fit, how do you care for it?
  3. Use metafields and product attributes for material, dimensions, compatibility and other specifications instead of hiding them in images.
  4. Keep variants tidy: one product with clear size and colour options, not five near-duplicate products.
  5. Add GTINs/barcodes where you have them so agents can match your product with confidence.
  6. Publish policies in plain language and add them to Shopify’s knowledge base so agents repeat the correct return and shipping rules.
  7. Collect reviews and keep them on your product pages.

Good news: everything that helps AI agents also helps Google Shopping, marketplaces and your own on-site search. It is work you only do once.

Do I still need a great website?

Yes. Many shoppers still discover a brand through an AI answer and then visit the store to learn more, check reviews or browse other products. Your website builds trust and brand; agentic channels add reach. The brands that win will do both well.

Where to start

How to measure results

Track orders by sales channel in Shopify analytics so you can see how much revenue comes from AI and other channels. Watch which products get picked up most often and use that to improve the rest of your catalog. Ask new customers how they found you; “an AI assistant recommended you” is becoming a common answer.

Common mistakes

  • Keyword-stuffed titles that read badly for both people and AI.
  • Missing or inconsistent prices between your store, Google Merchant Center and marketplaces.
  • Vague return policies that make agents, and shoppers, hesitate.
  • Out-of-stock products that stay visible and frustrate customers.

Frequently asked questions

What is agentic commerce?

It is shopping done with the help of AI agents that search, compare and buy products on the shopper’s behalf, often inside a chat with an AI assistant.

Do I have to pay extra to appear in AI shopping channels?

Availability and terms depend on the channel and your plan. Check the sales channels section of your Shopify admin to see which AI channels your store can use today.

Can small stores compete with big brands in AI search?

Yes. AI agents care about relevance and clear information, not ad budgets. A small store with excellent product data can be recommended ahead of a larger brand with thin descriptions.
