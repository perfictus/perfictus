Thank You & Order Status Pages: What the 2026 Checkout Upgrade Means for Your Store

Perfictus Shopify TeamNovember 2, 2026 2 min read

Non-Plus Shopify stores moved to the new Thank you and Order status pages in August 2026. Here is what changed and how to fix tracking and upsells.

Shopify has spent several years moving every store to checkout extensibility, a faster and more secure way to customise checkout. Shopify Plus stores had to upgrade their Thank you and Order status pages in 2025. For stores on Basic, Shopify and Advanced plans, the deadline was August 26, 2026, after which Shopify upgraded remaining stores automatically.

What changed

  • The old Additional scripts box on the order status page no longer runs.
  • Apps that injected code into these pages with ScriptTags stopped working there.
  • Customisations are now added with checkout UI extensions (app blocks) in the checkout editor.
  • Tracking moved to customer events and pixels, Shopify’s privacy-friendly way to track conversions.

Common problems we see

  • Missing conversions in Google Ads, Meta or GA4 because the old purchase snippet was in Additional scripts.
  • Post-purchase upsells or surveys that disappeared after the upgrade.
  • Duplicate purchase events when both an app pixel and a custom pixel fire.

How to fix it

  1. Open Settings → Checkout and review what is shown on the Thank you and Order status pages.
  2. Use official sales-channel apps (Google & YouTube, Facebook & Instagram) for ad tracking where possible.
  3. Recreate any other tracking as a custom pixel under Settings → Customer events.
  4. Reinstall upsell, survey or loyalty widgets as app blocks and position them in the checkout editor.
  5. Place a test order and confirm each conversion fires once, with the right value.

Compare your ad platforms’ reported purchases with your Shopify orders for the same week. A big gap usually means tracking needs attention.

Ideas to get more from the new pages

  • Post-purchase surveys: ask “How did you hear about us?” to improve your marketing.
  • Upsells and cross-sells for related products or add-ons.
  • Loyalty sign-up and referral offers while the customer is happiest.
  • Clear delivery information and links to track the order.
  • WhatsApp or support contact for quick questions.

Frequently asked questions

Did my store get upgraded automatically?

If you hadn’t upgraded by August 26, 2026, Shopify moved your store to the new pages automatically. Your old Additional scripts and ScriptTag-based code on those pages no longer run.

Why are my ad conversions lower since August?

The most common reason is that a purchase tracking snippet lived in Additional scripts. Rebuild it as a custom pixel or use the official sales-channel app for that ad platform.

Can I still customise the Thank you page?

Yes, with app blocks and checkout UI extensions added in the checkout editor. It is safer and more flexible than the old approach.

The new pages load faster, are more secure and keep working when Shopify updates checkout. If your tracking or upsells have been off since August, Perfictus can audit and rebuild them for you.
