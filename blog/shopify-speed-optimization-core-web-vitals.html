Shopify Speed Optimization: A Core Web Vitals Guide for 2026

Perfictus Shopify TeamOctober 15, 2026 2 min read

A slow Shopify store costs you sales and rankings. Here is how to measure Core Web Vitals and the fixes that make the biggest difference.

Shoppers expect pages to load almost instantly, especially on mobile. Speed affects conversion rates, ad costs and SEO. Google measures real-user experience with Core Web Vitals, and they are worth understanding.

The three Core Web Vitals

  • LCP (Largest Contentful Paint): how quickly the main content, often the hero image, appears. Aim for 2.5 seconds or less.
  • INP (Interaction to Next Paint): how quickly the page responds when someone taps or clicks. Aim for 200 milliseconds or less.
  • CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift): how much the layout jumps around while loading. Aim for 0.1 or less.

How to measure

Use the Web performance reports in your Shopify admin, Google Search Console’s Core Web Vitals report and PageSpeed Insights. Focus on real-user (field) data for mobile, because that is what Google uses.

The fixes that matter most

  1. Audit your apps. Uninstall unused apps and remove leftover code they left in your theme.
  2. Optimise the hero image. Serve correctly sized images and don’t lazy-load the first image on the page.
  3. Limit heavy sliders and videos above the fold.
  4. Reserve space for images, banners and app widgets to prevent layout shift.
  5. Reduce third-party scripts such as chat widgets, trackers and pop-ups, or load them later.
  6. Use a modern theme. Online Store 2.0 and Horizon-style themes are built for performance.
  7. Keep fonts lean: fewer font families and weights.

Test changes one at a time on a duplicate theme so you know exactly what helped.

Speed is a habit, not a project

Quick wins you can do today

  1. Delete apps you installed for a test and never removed.
  2. Replace autoplay homepage videos with a static image.
  3. Compress your largest images before uploading.
  4. Remove unused fonts and font weights from theme settings.
  5. Turn off pop-ups on mobile or delay them by a few seconds.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good speed score for Shopify?

Focus less on a single lab score and more on passing Core Web Vitals with real-user data: LCP under 2.5 seconds, INP under 200 milliseconds and CLS under 0.1.

Do apps slow down my store?

Some do, especially apps that load scripts on every page. Modern apps that use theme app extensions tend to be lighter and are removed cleanly when uninstalled.

Will a faster store really increase sales?

Faster pages reduce bounce rates and make shopping easier, especially on mobile. Speed improvements also support better rankings and lower ad costs.
