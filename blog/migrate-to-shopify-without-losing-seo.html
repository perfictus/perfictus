Migrating to Shopify Without Losing SEO: A Step-by-Step Guide

Perfictus Shopify TeamOctober 6, 2026 2 min read

Moving from WooCommerce, Magento or Wix to Shopify? Here is how to migrate products, customers and orders and keep your Google rankings.

Brands move to Shopify for reliability, speed, security and a huge app ecosystem. The biggest fear is losing the Google rankings you have built over years. With the right plan, you can migrate without a lasting drop in traffic.

Step 1: Benchmark before you move

  • Export your top pages by traffic from Google Search Console and analytics.
  • Crawl your current site to list every URL, title and meta description.
  • Record current rankings for your most important keywords.

Step 2: Migrate your data

Move products, variants, images, collections, customers, orders, blog posts and pages. Use Shopify’s import tools or a migration app, then check a sample of products by hand for missing images, variants or metafields.

Step 3: Map every old URL

Shopify uses fixed URL patterns such as /products/, /collections/ and /pages/. Create a spreadsheet that maps each old URL to its new Shopify URL, then import the list into URL redirects so every old link returns a 301 redirect.

Redirects are the single most important step for SEO in a migration. Don’t launch without them.

Step 4: Keep your on-page SEO

  • Carry over page titles, meta descriptions and headings for your top pages.
  • Keep valuable content; don’t replace detailed category text with a blank collection.
  • Recreate structured data, image alt text and internal links.

Step 5: Launch and monitor

  1. Point your domain to Shopify and confirm the primary domain.
  2. Submit the new sitemap in Google Search Console.
  3. Watch for crawl errors and 404s daily for the first two weeks.
  4. Compare traffic and rankings with your benchmark.

Migration mistakes to avoid

  • Launching without redirects, the number one cause of lost traffic.
  • Deleting old content that was bringing in visitors.
  • Changing everything at once: design, URLs, content and domain on the same day.
  • Forgetting images and alt text, which matter for image search.
  • Not testing on mobile before going live.

Frequently asked questions

Will I lose my Google rankings when moving to Shopify?

Some short-term movement is normal. With 301 redirects, preserved content and a fast new store, rankings usually recover within weeks and often improve.

Can I keep my existing domain?

Yes. You connect your existing domain to Shopify, so customers and search engines keep finding you at the same address.

Can customer passwords be migrated?

Passwords can’t be copied between platforms for security reasons. Customer accounts can be imported and invited to activate them, or use Shopify’s customer accounts sign-in by email code.

A small, short-term fluctuation is normal; a well-planned migration usually recovers quickly and then grows thanks to a faster store. Perfictus has migrated stores from WooCommerce, Magento, Wix and custom platforms with redirects and SEO handled end to end.
