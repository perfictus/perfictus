Shopify Sidekick in 2026: Practical Ways to Run Your Store with AI

Perfictus Shopify TeamOctober 24, 2026 2 min read

Sidekick, Shopify’s AI assistant, got much more capable in 2026. Here are practical ways to use it to save hours every week.

Sidekick is the AI assistant built into the Shopify admin. In 2026 it became far more capable: it can answer questions about your store’s data, draft content, set up discounts, write code and stage multi-step changes for you to review and approve.

10 practical ways to use Sidekick

  1. Sales questions: “What were my top 10 products by revenue last month?”
  2. Customer insights: “Which customers spent the most this year but haven’t ordered in 90 days?”
  3. Discount setup: “Create a 15% discount on the Winter collection for this weekend.”
  4. Product descriptions: draft SEO-friendly descriptions you then edit for brand voice.
  5. Collections: build automated collections from rules you describe in words.
  6. Email drafts: write the first version of a campaign or customer reply.
  7. Theme tweaks: generate a new theme block from a description.
  8. Reports: create a custom report without learning a query language.
  9. Store setup help: ask how to configure shipping zones, taxes or markets.
  10. Bulk tasks: stage changes across many products and review them before publishing.

Using Sidekick safely

  • Always review staged changes before approving them, especially prices and discounts.
  • Edit AI-written content so it sounds like your brand and is factually correct.
  • Test code changes in a duplicate theme first.
  • Keep humans in charge of anything customer-facing during peak sales.

Start with read-only questions like reports and insights. They save time instantly and carry no risk.

Where AI stops and experts start

Prompts that work well

Sidekick gives better answers when you are specific. Mention the time period, the products or customers you mean and the format you want back.

  • “Compare sales for my Winter collection this November with last November, by week.”
  • “List products with fewer than 10 units in stock that sold more than 20 units last month.”
  • “Draft a friendly email announcing free shipping this weekend, in under 120 words.”
  • “Create an automated collection of all products tagged ‘gift’ under 25 KWD.”

Frequently asked questions

Is Sidekick free?

Sidekick is built into the Shopify admin. Availability of specific features can depend on your plan, region and language, so check your admin to see what is available for your store.

Can Sidekick change my store without asking?

Sidekick prepares changes for you to review. You approve them before they take effect, so you stay in control.

Will AI replace my Shopify developer?

AI handles routine tasks faster, which frees developers to focus on custom features, integrations, performance and strategy, the work that makes your store stand out.

Sidekick is excellent for day-to-day work. Custom integrations, complex theme development, performance tuning and strategy still benefit from experienced Shopify developers. Perfictus works alongside your team so you get the best of both.
